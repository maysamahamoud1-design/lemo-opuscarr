# چرا؟ · ویتامین D (Dari): treatment

The Dari version of *Waayo? · Fiitamiin D*: the same story, cards, colours, watermark, loop and favourite-standard timing, translated into Dari and voiced by Azure `fa-IR-FaridNeural` (Persian, Iranian accent; Azure has no Dari voice) at +15 %. 1080×1920, 53.7 s.

How it is built: `tools/splice.py` makes the Somali film.js from the series engine and `cards.js`; `tools/dari.py` then turns it Dari: titles and labels translated, the cue words the cards are timed to mapped to the matching Dari words, Persian punctuation ends caption chunks, karaoke captions laid out right to left, and letter spacing removed (it breaks the joins between Persian letters). Persian letters come from Vazirmatn, declared inside the Montserrat and DM Mono families for the Arabic-script range only; `index.html` draws Persian strings right to left and everything else as before.

The Somali places and people stay (it is a translation, not a new film for Afghans abroad). The word rule "haweenka" is Somali-only and does not apply here.
