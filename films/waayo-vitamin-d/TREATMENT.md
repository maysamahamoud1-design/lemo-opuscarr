## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A list of causes**, one card each (sun, skin, latitude, clothes, indoors). Fast and clear, but it doesn't link anything to the body; the viewer gets facts, not a mechanism.
2. **One meter through the film (chosen).** A "D" meter (a capsule that fills with yellow) is introduced in scene 2, where sunlight on the skin fills it. Every later scene acts on that same meter: darker skin fills it more slowly (3), weak northern sun shrinks what reaches it (4), clothes and walls block it (5), an empty meter gives the symptoms (6), and the test tube and supplement bottle refill it (7). The question in scene 1 is answered by watching one gauge drain and refill.
3. **A journey**: one figure flies from Mogadishu to a northern city and we watch their sun drop. It's personal, but it would need more scenes than the user's eight and pushes the biology aside.

Choice 2: it keeps the user's eight scenes exactly and gives the film one thing to follow. In pictograms, a meter reads instantly at phone size.

## 2. Arc

Hook (0–7 s): plenty of sun in Somalia, so why the deficiency? Mechanism (7–19 s): sun on the skin makes D, which builds bone and muscle; darker skin needs more sun for the same amount. Turn (19–32 s): the weak sun of the UK, Norway and Canada, then clothes and walls take away what's left. Cost (32–38 s): tiredness, pain, soft bones (rickets). Fix (38–44 s): a blood test and supplements. End (44–51 s): the disclaimer, then "WAAYO?" and the question to the comments.

## 7. Facts

- Sun (UVB) on the skin makes vitamin D; it helps the body take up calcium for bones and muscles.
- Darker skin (more melanin) needs longer in the sun for the same amount.
- Above about 50° N (UK, Norway, most of Canada's population centres are 43–53° N), winter sun is too low for the skin to make vitamin D from about October to March.
- Covering clothes and indoor life cut exposure further.
- Low vitamin D: tiredness, bone and muscle pain; rickets (soft, bent bones) in children.
- UK public-health advice: everyone should consider a daily vitamin D supplement in autumn and winter, and people with dark skin or who cover up all year round. The film says to ask a doctor; it gives no doses.
